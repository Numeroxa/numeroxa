How much should you save each month?

Your required monthly savings depend on your goal, timeframe, and expected returns. Longer timeframes significantly reduce the monthly amount required.

Why starting early matters

Starting early allows compound interest to reduce how much you need to save each month.

Ways to reach your goal faster

  • Increase your contributions
  • Extend your timeframe
  • Earn higher returns (with appropriate risk)

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month?

Your monthly savings depend on your goal, timeframe, and interest rate.

Is it better to save or invest?

Saving is safer but offers lower returns, while investing can grow your money faster but involves risk.

What if I can’t reach my goal?

You can increase contributions, extend your timeframe, or adjust your target amount.

How much does interest help?

Interest can significantly reduce the amount you need to save over time.

Should I start saving early?

Yes—starting early allows compounding to reduce the total effort required.
