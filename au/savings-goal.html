How much should you save each month?

Your required monthly savings depend on your goal, timeframe, and expected returns. Longer timeframes reduce the amount required.

Why starting early matters

Starting early allows compound interest to significantly reduce how much you need to contribute.

Ways to reach your goal faster

  • Increase contributions
  • Extend timeframe
  • Improve investment returns

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month?

It depends on your goal, timeframe, and interest rate.

What if I can’t reach my goal?

You can increase contributions or extend your timeframe.

Does interest make a big difference?

Yes, compounding significantly reduces required savings.

Should I start early?

Yes, starting early has the biggest impact.
