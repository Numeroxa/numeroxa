How to reach your savings goal

Reaching a savings goal depends on how much you save, how long you save for, and the interest earned. This calculator shows how these factors work together to determine your required monthly contribution.

What affects your savings plan?

  • Your target amount
  • Your starting balance
  • Your timeframe
  • Your interest rate

Why starting early helps

The earlier you begin saving, the more time your money has to grow through compound interest, reducing the amount you need to contribute each month.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month?

It depends on your goal, timeframe, and expected return. This calculator provides a tailored estimate.

What if I can’t reach my goal?

You can increase contributions, extend your timeframe, or aim for higher returns.

Does interest make a big difference?

Yes, even modest interest rates can significantly reduce the amount you need to save.

Should I save or invest?

Savings are lower risk, while investments offer higher potential returns with more risk.

What happens if I start later?

You will need to contribute more each month to reach the same goal.

Is this calculator accurate?

It provides a reliable estimate based on standard compounding formulas.
