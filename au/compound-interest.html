What is compound interest?

Compound interest allows your investment to grow exponentially by earning returns on both your initial investment and accumulated gains.

Why time matters

The longer you invest, the more powerful compounding becomes, as returns generate further returns over time.

How to maximise growth

  • Start early
  • Invest consistently
  • Reinvest earnings

Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

It is interest earned on both your original investment and accumulated returns.

Does compounding frequency matter?

Yes, more frequent compounding leads to faster growth.

Why does compounding accelerate?

Because returns themselves generate additional returns over time.

Are regular contributions important?

Yes, consistent investing significantly increases long-term outcomes.
