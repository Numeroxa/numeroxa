What is compound interest?

Compound interest is the process where your investment earns returns not only on the original amount, but also on the accumulated interest over time. This creates exponential growth, especially over long periods.

Why compound interest is powerful

The longer your money stays invested, the more significant the compounding effect becomes. Early contributions can grow substantially compared to later deposits.

How regular contributions accelerate growth

Adding consistent monthly contributions significantly increases your final balance. Even small amounts can lead to large outcomes when combined with time and compounding.

What interest rate should you expect?

Investment returns vary depending on asset class. Historically, diversified portfolios have returned around 6–8% annually, but actual returns can fluctuate.

Key factors affecting compound growth

  • Time invested
  • Interest rate
  • Contribution frequency
  • Initial investment size
