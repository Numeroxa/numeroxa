What is compound interest?

Compound interest means you earn returns not only on your original investment, but also on the returns that accumulate over time.

Why compounding matters

The longer you invest, the more powerful compounding becomes, as your investment growth accelerates.

How to maximise returns

  • Start early
  • Invest consistently
  • Reinvest earnings

Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

It is interest earned on both your original investment and accumulated returns.

Why is compounding powerful?

Because returns generate further returns over time.

Do regular contributions help?

Yes, consistent investing significantly increases outcomes.

Does time matter?

Time is the most important factor in compounding growth.
