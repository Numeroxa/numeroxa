What is compound interest?

Compound interest is the process where your investment earns returns not only on your original amount, but also on the accumulated interest over time. This creates exponential growth.

Why compounding is powerful

The longer your money is invested, the more significant the compounding effect becomes. Small, consistent contributions can grow into large amounts over time.

Key factors affecting growth

  • Time – the most important factor
  • Interest rate – higher rates accelerate growth
  • Regular contributions – increase total returns

Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest in simple terms?

It’s interest earned on both your initial investment and previously earned interest.

How often should I contribute?

Regular contributions (monthly or weekly) significantly improve long-term outcomes.

Does starting early matter?

Yes, time has the biggest impact on compounding growth.

What rate of return should I use?

This depends on your investment type—shares typically return more than savings accounts but with higher risk.

Can I lose money with compounding?

Yes, if your investments decline in value, compounding can work in reverse.

Is compounding realistic?

Yes, it reflects real-world investment growth over time when returns are reinvested.
