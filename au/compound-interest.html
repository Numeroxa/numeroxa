What is compound interest?

Compound interest allows your money to grow exponentially by earning returns on both your initial investment and accumulated interest.

Why time is critical

The longer you invest, the more powerful compounding becomes.

How to maximise growth

  • Start early
  • Contribute regularly
  • Reinvest returns

Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

Compound interest means earning returns on both your initial investment and previously earned interest.

How often is interest compounded?

Most investments compound monthly or annually. More frequent compounding leads to faster growth.

Why does compounding take time to grow?

Compounding starts slowly but accelerates over time as interest earns interest.

Does contributing regularly make a big difference?

Yes, regular contributions significantly increase your final balance due to compounding.

What interest rate should I use?

It depends on your investment type—savings accounts are lower, while shares or ETFs typically have higher long-term returns.
