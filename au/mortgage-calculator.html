How mortgage repayments work in Australia

Mortgage repayments consist of both principal and interest. Early in your loan, most of your repayment goes toward interest, while later repayments reduce the principal more quickly.

Why extra repayments matter

Making extra repayments reduces your loan balance faster, which lowers the total interest you pay and shortens your loan term.

How to reduce your mortgage faster

  • Make regular extra repayments
  • Use offset accounts
  • Refinance to lower rates

Frequently asked questions

How are mortgage repayments calculated?

They depend on loan size, interest rate, and term.

What happens if I make extra repayments?

You reduce both total interest and loan duration.

Can I pay off my mortgage early?

Yes, additional repayments allow early payoff.

Does interest compound monthly?

Yes, most Australian mortgages compound monthly.
