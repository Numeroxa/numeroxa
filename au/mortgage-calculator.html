How mortgage repayments are calculated

Mortgage repayments are calculated using your loan amount, interest rate, and loan term. Each repayment includes both interest and principal.

What affects your mortgage repayments?

  • Loan size
  • Interest rate
  • Loan term

Why extra repayments matter

Extra repayments reduce total interest and shorten your loan term.
