How mortgage repayments are calculated

Mortgage repayments consist of both principal and interest. Early in the loan, most payments go toward interest.

Why extra repayments matter

Even small extra repayments can significantly reduce total interest and shorten your loan term.

Ways to pay off your mortgage faster

  • Make regular extra repayments
  • Refinance to a lower rate
  • Use offset accounts effectively
