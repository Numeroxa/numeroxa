How mortgage repayments are calculated

Mortgage repayments in Australia are typically calculated using an amortisation formula. Each repayment consists of both principal and interest, with the proportion changing over time.

Why interest matters

Even a small change in interest rates can significantly affect your total repayment amount over the life of the loan. Higher rates mean more of your early repayments go toward interest rather than reducing the principal.

Benefits of extra repayments

Making extra repayments can reduce both the total interest paid and the length of your loan. Even small additional payments each month can result in substantial savings over time.

Fixed vs variable interest rates

Fixed-rate loans offer certainty in repayments, while variable rates may fluctuate with market conditions. Choosing between them depends on your financial situation and risk tolerance.

How to reduce your mortgage faster

  • Make regular extra repayments
  • Use an offset account
  • Refinance to a lower rate
  • Increase repayment frequency
