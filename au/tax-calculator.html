How income tax works in Australia

Australia uses a progressive tax system, meaning income is taxed in brackets rather than a single flat rate.

What is the Medicare levy?

Most residents pay a 2% levy to fund public healthcare.

What is HECS/HELP?

Student loan repayments are calculated as a percentage of income above a threshold.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax should I pay?

It depends on income, residency, and deductions.

Why is my effective rate lower?

Because only part of your income is taxed at higher rates.

Do I have to pay Medicare levy?

Most residents do.

When do HECS repayments start?

Once income exceeds a threshold.
