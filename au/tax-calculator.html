How income tax works in Australia

Australia uses a progressive tax system, meaning higher portions of income are taxed at higher rates rather than one flat rate.

What affects your tax

  • Income level
  • Deductions
  • Residency status
  • Student loan repayments

How to reduce your tax

  • Claim work-related deductions
  • Use tax offsets
  • Contribute to super
